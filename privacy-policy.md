# Nightly Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

Nightly ("the app", "we", "us") is made by Adam Bourbia, New York, United States. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. The best way to reach us is by email: abourbia01@gmail.com.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your email address, password (stored encrypted by our database provider, never visible to us), username, display name, the date you accepted these terms, and your confirmation that you are of legal drinking age.

**What you post.** Chat messages, photos, reactions, your profile photo, and crew photos. These are visible only to members of the crew they were posted in.

**What you log.** The crews you join, the nights you take part in, the drinks you log (type and time), team assignments, and the names of bars you add to a night.

**Location: city only.** When you start a night, you can let the app look up your city. Your phone works out the city name, and only that name (for example "Brooklyn, New York") is saved. Your exact location is never stored or sent to us.

**Bar search.** When you search for a bar, the text you type and the night's city name are sent to Photon (photon.komoot.io), a free OpenStreetMap search service, to find matching places. We save the bar's name, its OpenStreetMap ID and its neighborhood. Your location is not sent.

We do not collect contacts, advertising identifiers, or health data, and we don't use analytics or ad trackers.

## How we use it

- To run the app: sign you in, show your crews, nights, chat, photos, stats, throwbacks and recaps.
- To send you account emails (sign-up and password-reset codes).
- To keep the app safe: reviewing reports of abusive content and acting on them.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

## Who can see your data

- **Your crews.** Members of a crew can see what's posted and logged in that crew.
- **Service providers.** Our database, login and file storage are provided by Supabase, which stores data on our behalf. Bar searches go to Photon as described above. Account emails (sign-up and password-reset codes) are sent through an email delivery service on our behalf.
- **The law.** We may disclose information if legally required.

## Blocking and reporting

You can block anyone, which hides their messages and photos from you. You can report messages and photos. Reports, including a copy of the reported content, are kept so they can be reviewed.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in **Profile → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your profile, messages, photos, reactions, drinks and bars. Crews where you were the only member are deleted. Nights and crews you started stay for the other members, without your name. Copies inside reports may be kept for up to 90 days to deal with the report.

## Age

Nightly is only for people of legal drinking age where they live (21+ in the United States). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If you believe someone underage has an account, contact abourbia01@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this you can do in the app. For anything else, email abourbia01@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
